Popular Destinations

Explore our handpicked selection of breathtaking destinations in Uganda

1. Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Famous for mountain gorilla trekking. A UNESCO world heritage site with dense tropical rainforest. Home to nearly half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas.

Book Trip

2. Queen Elizabeth National Park

Known for tree-climbing lions in the Ishasha sector. Boat cruises along the Kazinga Channel with hippos and birds. One of the most visited parks in Uganda.

Book Trip

3. Murchison Falls National Park

Uganda's largest national park. Famous for the spectacular Murchison Falls where the Nile squeezes through a narrow gorge. Popular for game drives and boat safaris.

Book Trip

4. Rwenzori Mountains National Park

Known as the "Mountains of the Moon." A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for mountaineering and glacier trekking.

Book Trip

5. Mount Elgon National Park

Ideal for hiking and nature exploration. Features caves, waterfalls, and unique vegetation.

Book Trip

6. Jinja - Source of the Nile

Known as the Adventure Capital of East Africa. Located at the source of the River Nile. Famous for white-water rafting, kayaking, and bungee jumping.

Book Trip

About MADVAN TRAVEL & TOURS UG

Founded with a passion for exploration and cultural exchange, MADVAN TRAVEL & TOURS UG has been creating unforgettable travel experiences for over 15 years.

MADVAN TOURS AND TRAVEL is a professional tour operating company dedicated to providing memorable travel experiences across Uganda and the wider East Africa region. The company specializes in organizing well-planned safaris, guided tours, and travel services that enable visitors to explore the region's rich wildlife, breathtaking landscapes, diverse cultures, and unique attractions.

Our team is committed to delivering reliable, safe, and enjoyable travel experiences through professional tour guiding, carefully designed itineraries, and quality customer service. Whether it is a wildlife safari, cultural tour, adventure trip, educational visit, or leisure travel, every journey is organized with attention to detail to ensure a rewarding experience.
